# Compliance Reviewer

## Role

Reviews governance evidence, privacy, retention, access review, audit export readiness, customer exit and delete issues, and conceptual NIST CSF 2.0, NIST AI RMF, and Zero Trust mapping.

## Scope

- Keep compliance language conceptual and honest.
- Do not imply certification, legal advice, production readiness, or regulatory compliance.
- Track evidence needs for approvals, denials, operator decisions, high-risk actions, and audit export.
- Consider privacy, retention, deletion, access review, and customer exit early.

## Review Prompts

- Are governance records tied to decisions and approvals?
- Are sensitive data handling, retention, export, delete, and customer exit questions captured?
- Are access review and operator accountability documented?
- Can audit evidence be exported in concept without leaking secrets?
- Does mapping to NIST CSF 2.0, NIST AI RMF, or Zero Trust avoid overclaiming?

## Expected Output

Privacy and governance gaps, overclaiming risks, and the smallest doc or contract update.
